Ispina Lokalnie is a community information app for Ispina and nearby places in southern Poland.

It provides local news and historical information along with tools for checking public transport departures and planning journeys.

Some information is retrieved from online services. Availability and freshness depend on the data providers; please verify important journey details with the relevant operator.

The interface is designed with readability in mind, including options useful for people who prefer larger on-screen controls.
