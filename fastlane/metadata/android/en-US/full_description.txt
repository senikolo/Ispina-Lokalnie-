Ispina Lokalnie Live brings together local information and tools for residents and visitors of Ispina, in Drwinia municipality, Poland.

Features:
* Trip planning for MLD, municipal transport and rail connections
* Local timetables and departure information
* Local news, village history and historical facts
* Weather forecast
* A senior-friendly display
* Radio and video content provided by external services

Some features download data from the Internet and depend on the availability and terms of external services.
